# Memorie — Support

Memorie is a private place to keep the memories of a life — yours, or
someone you love. Write a line about today, photograph the thing you'll
forget by Friday, or hold the microphone and just say it.

**Questions, problems, or an idea for it? Email
[reddysrinidhi70@gmail.com](mailto:reddysrinidhi70@gmail.com).** It's read
by the person who makes the app, and you'll usually hear back within a
couple of days.

## Common questions

### Where are my memories kept?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. There's no account to make and nothing
is uploaded in the background. That's also the catch: if the phone is
lost, the memories go with it unless something has a copy.

Two things guard against that:

- **iCloud backup.** Memorie's data is included in your iPhone's backup.
  Worth checking yours is on: *Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud
  Backup*.
- **Save a copy.** *Settings → Save a copy* writes everything into a
  single zip you can keep anywhere — a page any web browser can open,
  your photographs and voice recordings as files, and the same thing
  again in a form other software can read.

### How do recaps work?

Tap **Recaps**, choose a stretch of time, and Memorie writes it up as a
page of prose from your own entries and nothing else. It needs a network
connection for that one feature; everything else works offline.

The memories in the range you picked are sent to be written up and then
discarded. What's sent and what never leaves your phone is set out in the
[privacy policy](./).

The free version writes one recap a month.

### Can I get my memories out of the app?

Yes — *Settings → Save a copy*, as above. The page it produces carries the
photographs inside it, so it still shows them if you email it to yourself
or move it to another computer.

### What happens to videos?

Memorie doesn't copy them. It keeps a reference to the film in your Photos
library and plays it from there, so adding a video doesn't double the
space it uses. Delete it from Photos and it goes from Memorie too. In a
saved copy, videos are listed with their date and what you wrote, and the
film stays in Photos.

### Are my voice notes uploaded?

No. They're transcribed on the device, and the recording itself never
leaves it. Only the resulting text can be included in a recap, and only
when you ask for one.

### How do I delete everything?

*Settings → Delete everything.* It removes every person, memory,
photograph, voice note and recap on the phone. There's no account and no
copy on our side, so that deletes them completely — an iCloud backup taken
before then is the only way back.

### Can I keep timelines for more than one person?

This version keeps one. More people are coming in a later update.

### It's not working as expected

Send an email with what happened, what you expected instead, your iPhone
model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you have one. That's usually
enough to find it.
